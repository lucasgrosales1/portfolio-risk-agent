Rebalancing Policy

The portfolio is reviewed at least semi-annually and rebalanced toward the target when any asset class drifts more than 3 percentage points from its target weight.

Rebalancing sales are sourced tax-efficiently: from tax-sheltered accounts first (no current tax), then from tax-lots carrying losses or long-term gains before short-term gains. The tax cost of any taxable rebalancing is estimated and weighed before acting.

Structured-Product Policy

Satellite sleeve capped at 15% of the portfolio.

Approved structured products are held only as a satellite sleeve within the cap above, never as core holdings. Each carries issuer credit risk, limited liquidity, and a defined term.

  • Income / autocallable note — Approved. All four suitability gates pass. An income note can supplement cash flow: an illustrative 8% contingent coupon while the underlying stays above 70% of its start, over 2 years. The coupon is contingent, not guaranteed, and principal is at risk below the 60% barrier — this is not a bond.
  • Defined-outcome / buffered ETF — Approved. The client wants growth participation but has a 20% drawdown tolerance. A buffered ETF fits: an illustrative 15% downside buffer with upside capped near 16% over 1 year. Exchange-traded and liquid, with no single-issuer credit risk — the preferred way to add a buffer.
  • Buffered growth note — Approved. Optional: a buffered *note* offers a larger illustrative buffer (20%) and a longer 2-year defined term than the ETF, suitable given the client's liquidity and sophistication. It carries single-issuer credit risk and is illiquid — the ETF is preferred unless the longer term is wanted.
  • Principal-protected note — Excluded. Evaluated and declined — the client's drawdown tolerance is high enough that principal protection's opportunity cost and lockup aren't warranted.
